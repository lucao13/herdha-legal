Privacy Policy

Updated 13 Nov 2026

1. Who is responsible

HERDHA is an app for logging training, cardio and physical progress. The controller of your personal data is the app's developer, who is also the contact for any request about your data: herdhaapp@hotmail.com.

2. Data we collect

Account: email and password (the password is only stored in hashed form, never as plain text).

Profile: name, date of birth, height and goal.

Health and body data: weight, body fat percentage, measurements, water log, progress photos and profile photo.

Sleep (optional): if you connect it, we read the hours and stages of your sleep from Apple Health (synced by your watch) and store one row per night, plus the Garmin score you type. We only read: we never write anything to Health. You can erase this data at any time under Privacy.

App usage: workouts, sets, weights, cardio, routines and language preference.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or tracking data, and we use no analytics or advertising tools.

3. What we use it for, and the legal basis

We use your data to make the app work: storing and showing your workouts, charts, measurements and photos (performing the service you asked for).

Health data and body photos are sensitive personal data. We only process them with your specific consent, given when you enter the app, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data or your account.

We also use minimal data for account security and fraud prevention (legitimate interest) and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your photos

Progress photos are kept in private storage tied to your account and are only accessible to you, while signed in, through temporary links. We do not use your photos for face recognition, identification, AI training, advertising or any automated analysis, and we never show them to other users.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

We use Supabase as our infrastructure provider (database, authentication and file storage), with data hosted in the São Paulo, Brazil region.

Subscriptions: Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm the status of your subscription; it receives your account's internal identifier and your App Store purchase history, but not your name or your workout or health data.

Coach: if you connect with a coach in the app, that coach can see the workouts, cardio and history the connection gives access to. You can end the connection at any time.

Service providers may process data outside Brazil and Australia, always under contractual safeguards consistent with the LGPD and the Australian Privacy Act 1988.

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only access its own data (per-user access control in the database). Your sign-in on the device is kept in the system's secure keychain. No system is completely immune to failure; if an incident that may affect you occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

7. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your data and files are erased immediately from live systems; the provider's backup copies may persist for a limited period and are then discarded.

8. Your rights

Under the LGPD and the privacy laws where you live (such as the Australian Privacy Act), you may confirm that we process your data, access it, correct it, request deletion, request portability, learn who we share it with, and withdraw consent.

In the app, under Settings > Privacy, you can export your data, delete your workout data and permanently delete your account. For anything else, write to herdhaapp@hotmail.com.

9. Minors

HERDHA is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we will show a new notice in the app and ask for your agreement again.
